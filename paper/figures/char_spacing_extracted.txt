An abnormal Tc (character-spacing) operator widens
every inter-glyph gap past the glyph width itself.

poppler pdftotext  ->  word boundaries destroyed,
every glyph read as its own token:

  [ F e d e r a l
  R e g i s t e r
  V o l u m e
  8 9 ,
  R e g u l a t i o n s ]
  [ P a g e s

pypdf, which reconstructs words from glyph positions
and ignores the Tc operator, is unaffected:

  [Federal Register Volume 89, Number 143 (Thursday, July 25,

  pdftotext token-F1 = 0.017    pypdf token-F1 = 1.000
